Motor Self-Test

Runs a 3-second motor profile. Ensure track is clear.

Motor Calibration

Measure Armature Resistance (R).

  1. Ensure loco is on powered track.
  2. HOLD THE WHEELS FIRMLY to prevent rotation (STALL).
  3. Press Measure. The motor will hum for 1 second.

Manual Measurement

Alternatively, measure resistance directly across Motor pins (18 & 19) or commutator with decoder removed.
Enter result in CV 149 (1 unit = 0.01 Ohms). E.g., 10.5 Ohms = 1050.
